Generate ONE portrait composition concept for the Light theme.

This is the mobile counterpart of the APPROVED desktop-concept-v2.png.

OUTPUT

Create one portrait image at 780 × 2300 pixels,
representing a CSS composition of 390 × 1150
at 2x resolution.

Target aspect ratio: 390:1150.

Keep the full image uncropped.

The image must be a vertically composed illustrated
room, not a resized or cropped desktop screenshot.

No transparency required.

COMPOSITION PRINCIPLE

Recompose the room vertically while preserving
the same architectural world and approximate camera
language as desktop v2.

The mobile experience must clearly read as three
connected architectural zones:

TOP:
Logo mounting architecture and environment.

MIDDLE:
Washing machine and raccoon activity vignette.

BOTTOM:
Integrated project board with a large clean surface
for real dynamic UI.

All three zones belong to ONE continuous room.

Do not create three disconnected illustrations,
three separate cards or a vertical dashboard.

CAMERA AND PERSPECTIVE

- Nearly frontal 2.5D illustration.
- Upright architectural verticals.
- Modest downward viewing angle.
- Subtle furniture top/side visibility.
- Consistent perspective from top to bottom.
- No isometric dollhouse camera.
- No fisheye or wide-angle distortion.
- No stretched or disproportionately scaled objects.
- Preserve the shape language of desktop v2.

ARCHITECTURE

Use the same warm cream plaster, honey oak structural
beams and wood plank flooring.

Preserve visual continuity through:

- structural posts,
- wall planes,
- shelving,
- door/window framing,
- hanging foliage,
- timber supports,
- believable overlaps and cast shadows.

Include a partial doorway or window showing a
distant town/garden environment.

The exterior must remain secondary and must not
replace the room architecture.

Decorations should support depth, not clutter
critical interaction surfaces.

VERTICAL LAYOUT
Coordinates below use 390 × 1150 CSS proportions.
They are approximate composition zones, not rigid
pixel-perfect placement.

ZONE A — TOP / BRANDING
Approximate y: 0–240.

Include upper room architecture, a modest beam,
partial window/door structure, greenery and wall.

Reserve a clear visual wall area for the existing
unchanged SVG brand logo.

Do not draw the logo.

Keep enough room for future responsive global controls
(language, theme, settings) near the upper viewport edge.

Do not draw any controls.

Avoid large lamps, beams or foliage crossing the
reserved logo and controls areas.

The top should feel architectural and welcoming,
not like a conventional app header.

ZONE B — WASHER AND RACCOON
Approximate y: 235–570.

Create a compact but clearly recognizable activity
vignette featuring the SAME washer identity and
SAME original raccoon as desktop v2.

The washer remains:

- sage enamel,
- rounded rectangular body,
- readable circular front door,
- matte drawn surfaces,
- believable floor contact.

Keep the center of its front door circular area clean
and neutral.

This area will later receive a project thumbnail
inside a real runtime mask.

Do not paint a project, scenery or sample thumbnail
inside the door.

Place the raccoon beside the washer, preferably
on its right when composition allows.

Raccoon:

- compact,
- round head,
- soft gray-olive/cream body,
- dark-brown outlines,
- rounded eye masks,
- small paws,
- striped tail,
- curious expression,
- original 3/4 pose,
- looking toward the washer.

Feet and tail must be grounded correctly.

The raccoon must not be pasted over the machine
or enlarged into an unrelated cartoon sticker.

Washer and raccoon should feel like an intentional
activity cluster.

Use a small amount of shelving, towels or foliage
as architectural support.

Do not clutter the washer door or obscure the raccoon.

ZONE C — PROJECT BOARD
Approximate y: 555–1090.

Build an architectural wooden project board or
mounted panel that feels physically supported.

It may extend downward as a wall-attached structure
or a grounded built-in wooden board.

Keep the same oak construction and cream inset
surface as desktop v2.

The board must be visually integrated with the room,
not floating and not resembling a generic app card.

IMPORTANT:
The interior must be visually clean, almost frontal,
rectangular, and minimally distorted.

Target usable inner width:
approximately 342 CSS pixels at a 390px viewport.

The composition should also be adaptable to
approximately 272 CSS pixels of usable inner width
at a 320px viewport.

Use narrow but believable structural framing.

Provide enough vertical clean area to later hold:

- project search,
- project sorting,
- one-column project list,
- project selection states,
- create/import entry points.

These are FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS FOR FUTURE UI,
not artwork to draw now.

Do not draw any actual UI.

Leave the board interior completely blank and calm.

Do not include decorative frames inside the board,
painted project slots, fake paper cards, or sample
Minecraft imagery.

Ensure the board remains usable for dynamic content,
including scrolling, long project names, and empty
or loading states.

The bottom of the composition should show real
architectural continuity, with wood supports,
floor contact and subtle environment detail.

RESPONSIVE INTENT

This artwork represents a 390px-wide mobile layout.

At 320px width:

- the room must remain proportionate,
- the board should retain enough width for real UI,
- the page may scroll naturally,
- no whole-desktop shrink strategy is allowed.

Do not place content so close to the sides that
small-screen cropping would remove the machine,
raccoon or board structure.

The future interface is built using editable DOM
and Figma layers, not baked into this image.

LIGHTING

Match desktop v2.

- Warm daylight from upper-left.
- Soft ambient fill.
- Subtle warm pendant light where appropriate.
- Shadows generally toward lower-right.
- Grounding/contact shadows.
- Consistent illumination across every vertical zone.

Do not create separate lighting treatments for
the three composition zones.

ART STYLE

Preserve the exact visual direction of desktop v2:

- Hand-drawn illustration.
- Matte painted materials.
- Soft irregular outlines.
- Restrained paper-like grain.
- Diffuse shading.
- Warm, muted surfaces.
- Clear simplified shapes.
- Consistent environmental and character rendering.

Avoid:

- glossy 3D,
- photorealism,
- high-detail PBR materials,
- mismatched mascot/furniture rendering,
- excessive bloom,
- plastic surfaces,
- generic vector dashboard styling.

- project thumbnails,

- UI icons,

Do not redraw the existing brand logo.

Do not create a large blank white page.

Do not arrange the washer, raccoon and board as
