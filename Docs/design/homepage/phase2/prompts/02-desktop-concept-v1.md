PHASE 2 — FULL-ROOM DESKTOP COMPOSITION TARGET V1
Create a single concept image for the Light-theme homepage art direction of “The Block Laundromat.” This is NOT a final runtime asset and NOT a Phase 3 asset master. It is a visual composition target only.

Use the reference images as follows:

- Image A, B, C, D are the four original room illustrations. Use them to study the successful qualities: complete room architecture, depth layering, density, material warmth, exterior openings, integrated scene composition, and how illustration and UI can belong to one world.
- Image E is the existing Light logo reference only. Do not redraw it, do not place it, and do not render any logo text. Only reserve a clean zone where the real logo will be inserted later in Figma.
- Image F is the rejected old Figma composition. Treat it as a negative reference. Avoid sticker-like scattered objects, large blank white/gray fields, floating panels, a fake floor strip, or a conventional dashboard dominating the scene.
- Image G, H, and I are the selected raccoon style references. Use them to build an original raccoon character language: rounded silhouettes, soft irregular dark-brown outlines, cream / gray-olive / warm-brown shape language, simple round eye masks and paws, restrained grain, and diffuse shading. Do not copy exact props, costumes, or poses from the references.

GOAL
Design a coherent full-room desktop composition in a hand-drawn illustrated style where the environment, furniture, and raccoon all share one unified material language. The room must feel like a real interior space, not a collage. It should support a future interactive homepage for project management.

STYLE + MOOD

- Light-theme room.
- Warm cream plaster walls.
- Honey oak beams and planked floor.
- Sage enamel washing machine.
- Terracotta and olive foliage accents.
- Daylight enters from the upper-left through an open doorway and/or window.
- Warm pendant lights provide subtle fill only.
- Cast shadows generally fall down-right.
- Soft diffuse shading, light paper-like grain, restrained texture.
- Avoid hyperreal 3D furniture. The furniture and room must feel drawn in the same world as the raccoon.
- Overall focal accents can use turquoise/green in the washer door ring and small UI-supportive accents, while the future logo area will later receive the colorful existing logo in Figma.

CAMERA

- Nearly frontal 2.5D room view.
- Gentle visible top planes and one side plane of furniture.
- Verticals remain upright.
- Modest downward view only.
- Not isometric.
- Not dollhouse.
- Not wide-angle.
- Keep camera logic and scale consistent across all objects.

COMPOSITION
Canvas size/aspect: 1672 × 941, landscape.

Build a complete room with clear architecture and three depth planes:

1. Foreground: grounded floor and a few cropped edge elements only.
2. Middle: washer activity cluster, shelving, wall, window, board.
3. Background: doorway/window view to a distant town or garden.

Include:

- Full visible room edges and ceiling beams.
- Open left doorway revealing distant exterior town/garden.
- Middle shelves/wall/window.
- Grounded foreground floor.
- Believable supports, overlaps, occlusion, and contact logic.
- No huge empty blank middle area.

LAYOUT ZONES (approximate, preserve hierarchy rather than exact pixel precision)

- Ceiling / upper architecture occupies roughly y 0..150.
- Open left doorway roughly x 20..260, y 180..730.
- Shelf zone roughly x 255..380, y 180..590.
- Reserve an EMPTY logo mounting zone on the wall roughly x 400..920, y 80..310. This zone must remain visually clear. Do not render any logo, title, or text there.
- Washing machine roughly x 390, y 350, width about 510, height about 480, with feet landing near y 830.
- A raccoon stands to the RIGHT of the washer, roughly x 850, y 570, width about 230, height about 285, feet near y 855, curious expression, 3/4 facing left toward the washer/center. Make this an original pose, not a copy from references.
- Project board structure roughly x 1020, y 235, width about 610, height about 575, with real support/feet/ledge connection down toward y 840. It must feel mounted and structurally supported, never floating.
- The board needs a clean inner usable rectangle roughly x 1060..1590 and y 300..740: straight, readable, minimally distorted, and visually calm for later DOM/Figma UI insertion.
- The washer door must contain a clean circular inset / masking region for a future selected-project thumbnail, but leave it blank or neutral in this concept. Do not paint a sample project preview inside it.
- Plant at left foreground roughly x 120, y 650, width about 190, height about 220, in an anchored pot, grounded properly, not overlapping future task-control regions.
- Allow a few cropped bench/plant/peripheral objects only at the extreme edges.

STRICT EXCLUSIONS

- No rendered logo.
- No title text.
- No words anywhere.
- No UI labels, project names, cards, search boxes, icons, buttons, sort controls, account text, sample thumbnails, or baked interface text.
- No fake project data on the board.
- No painted thumbnail inside the washer door.
- No conventional dashboard/header taking over the scene.
- No floating board disconnected from architecture.
- No sticker-like raccoon, washer, or plant.
- No big blank white/gray fields.
- No floor represented as just a colored strip.

VISUAL INTENT
The scene should clearly read as:

- A complete laundromat-like creative project room.
- Washer + raccoon + board as the main activity cluster.
- Enough structural richness to avoid the rejected sparse/sticker layout.
- Enough clean reserved surfaces to support future real UI.
- Unified lighting, consistent eye-line, coherent perspective, and grounded object contacts.
- A polished concept image that can guide Phase 2 art direction and later Figma composition work.

OUTPUT
Return one single desktop concept image only, 1672×941 ratio, fully illustrated, no transparency.

QUALITY BAR / ACCEPTANCE
The image should pass only if it shows:

1. A complete coherent room with clear architecture and three depth planes.
2. A consistent near-frontal 2.5D camera with mild top-plane visibility and upright verticals.
3. Unified material language across raccoon, washer, furniture, and architecture.
4. Clear usable reserved zones for:
   - empty wall logo area,
   - clean washer door inset,
   - clean board inner surface.
5. Strong grounding: believable supports, feet on floor, pot anchored, cast shadows and contact logic.
6. No sticker layout, no floating panel, no giant blank field, no conventional dashboard dominance.
7. A composition clearly richer and more integrated than the rejected old Figma reference.

If a trade-off is needed, prioritize whole-room coherence, depth, and reserved interactive surfaces over extra decoration.
