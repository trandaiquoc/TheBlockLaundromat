# Job 1A — replacement honey-oak material swatch

Generate one square, high-resolution, fully opaque honey-oak material texture.

Use the attached washer master as the reference for overall color warmth, matte finish, lighting softness and illustration quality. Use the raccoon master only as a reference for the shared hand-drawn illustration finish.

## Correct the material

- Warm, natural honey-oak, slightly muted rather than saturated orange.
- Fine, subtle, predominantly horizontal wood grain.
- Low-contrast grain with smooth tonal transitions.
- Very small, infrequent knots, preferably no prominent knots.
- Natural variation without large dark streaks or dramatic grain patterns.
- Consistent soft lighting across the entire surface.
- Matte, softly illustrated wooden appearance.

## Tiling requirement

Create a genuinely seamless, tileable texture. All four edges must transition naturally into their opposite edges without visible seams, abrupt color changes or discontinuities.

The texture must remain visually coherent when repeated horizontally and vertically.

## Strict exclusions

- No red or magenta fringe.
- No transparent pixels.
- No perspective or directional lighting gradients.
- No shadows or highlights suggesting a physical object.
- No tabletop, furniture, frame, board, fasteners, bevels or decorations.
- No text, logos or environmental elements.

## Output

One square, high-resolution, fully opaque material swatch. This is texture artwork only, not a finished asset or board.

Prioritize restrained grain and seamless repetition over decorative wood detail.
