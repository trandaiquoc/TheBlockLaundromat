OBJECT DESIGN

Create a physically supported wooden project board.

The object consists of two clearly defined parts:

A. PRIMARY BOARD PANEL
A broad near-frontal horizontal rectangle,
with a warm oak border and a clean cream-colored face.

B. SECONDARY SUPPORT STRUCTURE
Simple wooden posts and diagonal braces below the panel,
connected physically to its frame.

The primary panel controls the object's geometry.
The support structure must adapt to the panel,
never the other way around.

CRITICAL GEOMETRY

Design the primary panel FIRST.

Target relative dimensions:

Outer frame:
660 units wide × 435 units high.

Clean inner face:
560 units wide × 355 units high.

INNER FACE ASPECT RATIO:
560 / 355 = approximately 1.577.

The inner face must read as a WIDE LANDSCAPE RECTANGLE.

Its width is approximately 1.58 times its height.

It must NOT be:

- square,
- portrait-shaped,
- extremely wide and shallow,
- excessively tall.

The cream face is the dominant central feature.

Keep the full clean rectangular surface visible.

Maintain a nearly frontal camera so that
top and bottom widths are approximately equal.

The corners must form a stable rectangle.

No major perspective convergence.

The outer frame can contain modest illustrated
corner joints, bolts and frame thickness.

No hardware may protrude into the clean UI region.

SUPPORT STRUCTURE

Attach two sturdy outer posts to the side frame.

Extend them below the board panel.

Add a simple central support post or rear support
only if needed for physical credibility.

Small diagonal wooden braces may connect
the side posts to the lower frame.

Support geometry must:

- remain below or beside the clean face,
- connect to the board frame,
- have complete lower ends,
- appear structurally credible,
- never overlap the UI area,
- never change the panel aspect ratio.

The object must resemble a supported architectural
project board, not a floating poster or UI card.

Do not add a large separate cabinet or workstation.

CAMERA

Near-frontal illustrated 2.5D.

- Upright vertical posts.
- Almost parallel front face.
- Slight visible wood thickness.
- Very mild downward perspective.
- No isometric camera.
- No exaggerated foreshortening.
- No fisheye distortion.

MATERIALS

Honey oak outer structure.
Warm cream painted inner face.

Soft irregular dark-brown outlines.
Matte hand-drawn material.
Restrained wood grain.
Subtle paper-like texture.
Diffuse shading.

Warm daylight from upper-left.

Soft form shading toward lower-right.

Match the approved Light-theme environment
and production character/washer masters.

UI-SAFE FACE

The entire inner rectangle must remain calm,

TRANSPARENCY

CRITICAL:

Output must be a real RGBA PNG.

All pixels outside the wooden board silhouette
must have genuine alpha transparency.

The cream board face must be opaque.

The wooden frame, posts and braces must be opaque.

Do not paint or render:

- checkerboard transparency previews,
- white background,
- black background,
- colored background,
- floor,
- wall,
- room environment,
- cast floor shadow.

A checkerboard visible as actual PNG pixels
is a failure.

Do not simulate transparency with a texture.

Preserve clean exterior alpha edges.

No halo, matte fringe or rectangular residue.

CANVAS

Use a square or near-square transparent canvas
with enough height for the support structure.

Suggested output:
1600 × 1600 pixels.

The object should occupy most of the canvas
without cropping.

The canvas ratio does not determine
the board-face aspect ratio.

The board face must remain approximately 1.577
regardless of the canvas dimensions.

Do not stretch the final image to meet this ratio.
