PHASE 3 — DESKTOP BOARD SUPPORT MASTER 01

PROJECT
The Block Laundromat — Illustrated Interactive Homepage.

TASK

Generate ONE standalone architectural support structure
for the already approved PRIMARY PANEL MASTER v2.

The result must be a separate transparent PNG.

Do not redraw the approved panel.

REFERENCES

1. panel-master-v2.png
   PRIMARY GEOMETRY REFERENCE.

   Match the approved panel's physical width,
   frame geometry, perspective and intended
   mounting positions.

2. desktop-concept-v2.png
   Reference for the project board's architectural
   position, room depth and floor relationship.

3. washer-master-v1.png
   Approved material and lighting reference.

4. raccoon-master-v1.png
   Approved illustration and outline reference.

ASSEMBLY CONSTRAINT

The approved panel remains a separate foreground layer.

This support structure must sit BEHIND that panel
when the two PNGs are composed.

The panel itself must not be included in the output.

Do not create another cream board face,
another wooden rectangular panel or a second frame.

GEOMETRY

Desktop target scene:
1672 × 941 CSS pixels.

Approved primary panel:
approximately 660 × 434 CSS pixels.

Intended panel top:
approximately y = 250.

Intended panel bottom:
approximately y = 684.

Floor contact target:
approximately y = 840.

The support therefore needs visible structural
continuation beneath the panel, approximately
156 CSS pixels from panel bottom to floor contact.

These are scene coordinates, not output canvas
dimensions.

DESIGN

Create a simple physically credible honey-oak
support assembly.

Include:

- two principal vertical support posts,
- a restrained horizontal structural crossmember,
- short diagonal reinforcing braces where useful,
- complete lower ends suitable for floor contact,
- logical upper attachment sections that extend
  behind the approved panel.

The supports must align with the panel's side
construction and avoid looking like a second
unrelated piece of furniture.

The upper portions may be partially hidden
when the panel is placed in front.

No floating joints.

No detached braces.

No unexplained gaps between connected timbers.

PANEL COMPATIBILITY

The support should be designed for the approved
panel proportions, not for a new generated panel.

Use the panel as a measurement and perspective
reference.

The upper structure must be visually plausible
when hidden behind the approved panel.

All support pieces must remain outside the
panel's clean UI face in the final composition.

Do not duplicate the existing frame rails
or decorative corner bolts.

Do not create a foreground element that crosses
over the panel's usable surface.

PHYSICAL GROUNDING

The visible lower posts must form a stable
support structure.

The object must be capable of appearing grounded
against the existing illustrated room floor.

Include complete post bottoms.

Do not include the actual floor or cast floor shadow.

Contact shadows will be separate during assembly.

CAMERA

Match the approved panel:

- near-frontal 2.5D,
- upright verticals,
- mild depth visibility,
- very limited perspective convergence,
- no isometric projection.

All timber joints must share one consistent
camera orientation.

MATERIALS

Honey oak wood matching panel-master-v2.

- Warm muted wood palette.
- Soft dark-brown outlines.
- Restrained hand-drawn grain.
- Matte material finish.
- Diffuse shading.
- Upper-left daylight.
- Gentle lower-right form shading.

Avoid:

- glossy 3D,
- photorealism,
- overly detailed wood texture,
- heavy black outlines,
- exaggerated bevels.

ASSET OWNERSHIP

This asset owns ONLY the support structure.

Do not include:

- primary board face,
- board frame,
- second panel,
- washing machine,
- raccoon,
- plants,
- shelves,
- towels,
- lanterns,
- room walls,
- floor,
- project thumbnails,
- UI,
- logo,
- text.

TRANSPARENCY

Output genuine RGBA PNG.

All pixels outside the wood support silhouette
must have true alpha transparency.

Wooden parts remain opaque.

No:

- painted background,
- checkerboard texture,
- white or black background,
- edge halo,
- rectangular alpha residue,
- baked environmental shadows.

All structural pieces must remain fully visible.

OUTPUT

One high-resolution transparent PNG.

Choose canvas proportions suited to the complete
support structure.

Do not force a square canvas if it wastes space.

Allow modest transparent padding around the object.

Keep the entire structural silhouette inside the image.

No sprite sheet or multiple alternatives.

ACCEPTANCE

PASS only when:

1. The support matches the geometry and material
   language of panel-master-v2.

2. The approved panel itself is not reproduced.

3. Two primary posts and all braces are physically
   connected into one credible structure.

4. The support can extend from behind the panel
   down to the intended desktop floor contact.

5. Its visible lower continuation is proportionate
   to the approximately 156 CSS px scene gap.

6. No member crosses the clean UI surface
   after correct background-layer composition.

7. The structure has a consistent near-frontal
   illustrated perspective.

8. All exterior pixels use true transparency.

9. The asset has no floor, wall, UI, text or props.

10. Complete edges and contact ends are available
    for later composition.

11. The geometry permits physically credible
    mounting of the approved panel without
    stretching either asset.

12. The result does not resemble a second board,
    freestanding decorative poster or random
    disconnected timbers.

Generate exactly ONE desktop support master.
Do not generate an assembled room.
