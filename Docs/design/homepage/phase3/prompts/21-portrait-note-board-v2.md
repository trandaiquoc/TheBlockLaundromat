DESIGN INTENT

The visual identity is a lightweight wooden
NOTE BOARD attached to the room architecture.

It must feel like a thin framed display surface,
not a piece of heavy furniture.

The note board is the primary visual element.

The support shelf is a separate, slim horizontal
element above the board.

The two elements may share concealed mounting
brackets, but must remain visually distinct.

NOTE-BOARD CONSTRUCTION

Use a thin honey-oak frame surrounding
one flat cream-painted board.

Include:

- four narrow wooden rails,
- subtle joints,
- restrained small fasteners if appropriate,
- a credible thin profile.

Do not add:

- oversized corner caps,
- large decorative bolts,
- thick structural columns across the front,
- deep sidewalls,
- storage compartments,
- drawers,
- cabinet doors,
- enclosed furniture volume.

The frame must look light and functional.

SHELF CONSTRUCTION

Above the board, create one slim,
nearly horizontal oak support shelf.

The shelf provides a common platform for
two independently composited objects:

- approved washing machine,
- approved raccoon mascot.

Do NOT draw those objects.

The shelf needs:

- a stable horizontal upper surface,
- a readable narrow front edge,
- modest physical depth,
- believable structural support.

It should not resemble a thick countertop.

Keep the visible front thickness near
the geometry guide's 16-unit target.

MOUNTING AND SUPPORT

Use minimal rear brackets or slim wooden/metal
attachments linking the shelf and note board
to the future room architecture.

The board must not look like a floating sticker.

Any support posts must be slender and secondary.

If two lower contact extensions are required,
keep them behind the note board and extend
toward the guide's lower contact line.

Do not add large legs or build a cabinet frame.

Do not construct a closed box around the board.

All structural connections must be plausible.

The final room will provide the actual wall,
floor and environmental contact relationships.

CAMERA

Near-frontal illustrated 2.5D.

- Straight horizontal board rails.
- Upright vertical edges.
- Minimal perspective convergence.
- Slight top visibility on the slim shelf.
- Very limited frame thickness visibility.
- No isometric view.
- No dramatic foreshortening.

The note-board face must remain almost perfectly
frontal for future real DOM/Figma UI placement.

STYLE AND MATERIALS

Match the approved production masters.

- Warm honey-oak frame.
- Cream-painted note surface.
- Soft irregular dark-brown outlines.
- Matte, hand-drawn rendering.
- Restrained paper texture.
- Gentle wood grain.
- Soft diffuse shading.
- Daylight from upper-left.
- Subtle lower-right form shading.

Avoid glossy 3D, photorealism,
plastic materials or heavy bevels.

UI-SAFE FACE

The cream face must be completely blank.

It must support:

- search and sorting,
- one-column project list,
- create/import actions,
- selected and empty states,
- future 44px interaction targets.

These are functional requirements only.

DO NOT generate any UI.

No text, icons, buttons, cards,
thumbnails, labels or sample data.

Do not add decorative divisions.

TRANSPARENCY

Output a genuine RGBA PNG.

structural silhouette must have alpha 0.

The cream face and wooden pieces remain opaque.

No fake checkerboard.
No painted background.
No white matte.
No dark halo.
No colored fringe.
No rectangular residue.
No baked floor shadow.

Keep the complete structure visible,
with sufficient transparent padding.
