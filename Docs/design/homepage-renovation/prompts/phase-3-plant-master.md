## Prompt supplied by Check and sent unchanged to Generate

PHASE 3 — ASSET 04 — PLANT MASTER

Create ONE complete, standalone, original 2D potted houseplant illustration for the homepage of The Block Laundromat.

This is the fourth and final sample asset of Phase 3.

REFERENCE PRIORITY

Reference A — Approved Raccoon Master 004 Clean:
Use as the primary reference for soft organic shapes, natural dark-brown linework, warm muted colors, subtle texture and cozy hand-drawn storybook personality.

Reference B — Approved Washer Master 001:
Use as a reference for consistent outline weight, restrained flat shading, object readability and clean construction.

Reference C — Approved Project Board Master 002:
Use as a reference for muted earthy colors, subtle material texture and handcrafted visual character.

Reference D — Original The Block Laundromat logo, if supplied:
Use only for overall brand harmony. Do not recreate, modify, trace or include the logo.

Create an original plant design belonging to the same illustrated visual world as the three approved masters.

OBJECT

Exactly ONE complete potted Heartleaf Philodendron houseplant.

The plant should feel friendly, cozy, softly organic and suitable for a welcoming illustrated homepage.

Do not create a realistic botanical painting or a generic glossy vector plant icon.

PLANT SHAPE

- Compact, gently spreading foliage.
- Softly rounded, heart-shaped leaves.
- Approximately 7–10 prominent leaves.
- Varied leaf sizes and natural orientations.
- Approximately 3–4 visually distinguishable leaf groups.
- A few short visible stems connecting the foliage to the plant base.
- Natural but restrained organic asymmetry.
- Avoid perfectly mirrored or repetitive foliage.
- Avoid excessively long hanging vines.
- Keep the plant silhouette balanced and readable at small sizes.

Every leaf must be fully formed.

Leaves may overlap naturally, but their visible shapes must remain understandable.

No cut-off foliage, broken stems, incomplete branches or arbitrarily disconnected leaves.

STEMS AND CONNECTIONS

- Use a small number of clearly readable stems.
- Stems must connect naturally to the leaves and toward the soil inside the pot.
- Some stems may be hidden behind overlapping foliage, but no stem should terminate inexplicably in empty space.
- Do not create loose floating leaves.
- Do not add excessive botanical detail.

LEAF GROUPS

Design 3–4 main leaf groups with recognizable contours and slightly different orientations.

The groups should feel like one coherent living plant, not separate pieces pasted together.

Their shapes should be sufficiently distinct that a designer could later recreate the main groups as independent Figma layers for gentle leaf-sway animation.

IMPORTANT:
Generate one complete illustration only.
Do not generate separated parts, an exploded view, a collage or fake editable layers.

POT DESIGN

- One complete ceramic plant pot.
- Compact and softly rounded.
- Slightly wider near the opening.
- Gently rounded sides and a stable flat base.
- Subtle handmade irregularity.
- A clearly readable elliptical upper rim.
- Warm muted terracotta color, not bright orange.
- Simple flat shading with a darker side and slightly lighter upper-left highlight.
- Very subtle surface grain.
- No patterns, faces, labels, inscriptions or decorations.
- No saucer, wooden stand, pedestal or additional container.

SOIL

A simple dark muted-brown soil surface may be visible inside the upper opening of the pot.

The soil should appear naturally contained inside the rim.

Avoid detailed soil grains, stones, roots or decorative objects.

VIEW AND PROPORTIONS

- Mostly front-facing with a gentle three-quarter angle.
- Show the elliptical pot opening clearly.
- Keep the pot and foliage perspective consistent.
- Avoid exaggerated isometric perspective.
- Leaf canopy approximately 60% of overall plant height.
- Pot approximately 40% of overall height.
- Canopy moderately wider than the pot, approximately 1.3–1.5 times its width.
- All foliage, stems, pot rim and pot base must be visible within the image canvas.

These proportions are visual guidelines, not exact geometric constraints.

VISUAL STYLE

Cozy hand-drawn flat storybook illustration.

Match the approved Raccoon, Washer and Project Board masters.

- Soft dark warm-brown outlines.
- Slight natural variation in line thickness.
- Rounded, friendly, organic shapes.
- Gentle irregularities instead of perfect vector geometry.
- Warm desaturated palette.
- Flat color regions.
- Restrained shading using simple color shapes.
- Upper-left lighting direction.
- Extremely subtle fine-grain texture inside the colored areas.
- Clean readable contours.
- No photorealism.
- No glossy 3D rendering.
- No plastic-looking highlights.
- No heavy gradients.
- No heavy paper texture.
- No rough sketch lines or excessive hatching.

COLOR DIRECTION

Leaves:
Muted sage green, soft moss green and restrained olive green.

Leaf shadows:
Slightly darker green, without turning the foliage nearly black.

Stems:
Muted natural green and warm brown.

Pot:
Warm terracotta, clay brown and subtle beige highlights.

Outline:
Dark warm brown consistent with the approved masters.

Soil:
Dark muted earthy brown.

Maintain clear tonal separation between leaves, stems, soil and pot.

The green foliage should complement the original brand's Minecraft grass colors without copying their vivid saturation.

COMPOSITION

- Exactly one complete plant in one complete pot.
- The entire object must be visible.
- No cut-off leaves, stems, pot edges or base.
- No collage, patchwork, reconstructed missing regions or assembled fragments.
- Visually center the complete object.
- Leave balanced transparent space around the silhouette.
- Request a genuine transparent background.
- Maintain at least 5% transparent safe margin on all four canvas sides.
- Measure safe margin from every pixel with nonzero alpha, including very faint pixels.
- Do not place shadows, grain, stray pixels or effects outside the safe area.
- Keep clean anti-aliased edges.
- Aim for sufficient resolution for homepage artwork.

The output PNG dimensions, alpha validity and safe margins must be verified from the actual file after generation, not assumed from the request.

No washer.
No board.
No additional pots or plants.
No room.
No wall.
No floor.
No outdoor scenery.
No ground shadow or contact shadow outside the plant.
No decorative objects.
No Minecraft blocks.
No watering can.
No flowers or fruit.
No insects.
No text, numbers, icons or labels.
No logo.
No watermark.
No background color.
No artificial frame or border.
No cropped components.
No incomplete branches.
No floating foliage.
No photorealistic botanical textures.

ANIMATION READINESS

The plant may later support gentle leaf and stem motion on the homepage.

Therefore:
- Give the major leaf groups distinguishable silhouettes.
- Avoid overly tangled stems.
- Avoid excessive overlapping that prevents visual separation.
- Keep the connection between foliage and pot visually coherent.

Do not output fake layered files or disconnected pieces.

This deliverable is one complete visual master.

ACCEPTANCE TARGET

Produce an original, charming, complete potted houseplant that looks naturally compatible with the approved Raccoon Master 004 Clean, Washer Master 001 and Project Board Master 002.

The silhouette must be clear, the leaf groups readable, and the entire illustration independently usable on a transparent homepage canvas.

No reconstruction, patching, fragment assembly or repainting of missing regions should be required.

Generate the illustration only.
