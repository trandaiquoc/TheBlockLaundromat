## Prompt supplied by Check and sent to Generate

PHASE 3 — ASSET 03 — PROJECT BOARD MASTER

Create ONE complete, standalone, original wooden project board illustration for the homepage of The Block Laundromat.

REFERENCE PRIORITY

Reference A — Approved Raccoon Master 004 Clean:
Primary character-style reference for warm muted colors, soft hand-drawn brown outlines, organic imperfections and very subtle grain texture.

Reference B — Approved Washer Master 001:
Primary object-style reference for outline weight, clean readable geometry, flat shading, warm cream surfaces and consistent three-quarter perspective.

Reference C — Original The Block Laundromat logo:
Brand identity reference only. Preserve visual harmony without copying, recreating or inserting the logo.

Optional Instagram references:
Use only as broad inspiration for cozy hand-drawn storybook illustration. Never copy existing compositions or distinctive artwork.

OBJECT

Create exactly ONE complete wooden project board.

It must be a self-contained, fully visible, independent illustration, suitable as the decorative background surface for real, editable homepage UI components.

DESIGN DIRECTION

A charming, softly rectangular, landscape-oriented wooden notice board.

The board consists of:
1. One complete outer wooden frame.
2. One large, uninterrupted inner writing/display surface.
3. Subtle visible wooden thickness along the upper and right edges.
4. Minimal structural details that make the object recognizable as a handcrafted board.

No other objects are required.

GEOMETRY AND VIEW

- Landscape rectangle, approximately 1.5–1.7 times wider than tall.
- Mostly front-facing.
- Very gentle three-quarter perspective.
- Front surface must remain visually dominant.
- Show only a narrow amount of the top and right-side thickness.
- Avoid deep isometric projection.
- Softly rounded corners.
- Slight organic irregularity, but no warped or twisted geometry.
- Straight enough edges to support precise alignment of editable UI.
- All four frame corners and the entire perimeter must be clearly visible.
- No crop, missing sections, torn edges, broken planks or incomplete construction.

WOODEN FRAME

- Warm muted medium-brown wood.
- Rounded outer and inner frame edges.
- Modest visible thickness.
- A slightly darker brown outline.
- Very subtle handmade asymmetry.
- A few restrained natural wood-grain indications are acceptable on the frame only.
- No exaggerated wood texture, carved ornaments, metallic trim or decorative flourishes.
- Keep the frame moderately narrow to maximize usable inner space.

INNER BOARD SURFACE

- A single continuous matte warm-cream painted wooden panel.
- Smooth, light and visually quiet.
- Slight tonal variation is acceptable.
- Very subtle fine-grain texture consistent with the approved masters.
- No prominent plank seams, knots, stains, cracks or heavy wood grain.
- No internal compartments or permanently drawn UI areas.
- The surface must remain visually suitable for placing real text, controls, project cards and buttons above it later.

Target a generous usable area:
Approximately 75–82% of the overall board width and 70–80% of its overall height, without counting the surrounding wooden frame.

The central display area must be unobstructed.

VISUAL STYLE

Cozy hand-drawn flat storybook illustration.

Match the established visual family of Raccoon Master 004 Clean and Washer Master 001.

- Soft dark warm-brown outlines.
- Natural but restrained variation in line thickness.
- Rounded friendly shapes.
- Warm, desaturated colors.
- Mild organic imperfections without distorted structure.
- Flat color shading.
- Light from the upper-left direction.
- Extremely subtle grain confined to painted surfaces.
- No photographic texture.
- No glossy coating.
- No bevel or shiny 3D effects.
- No heavy drop shadows.

The board should feel handcrafted, welcoming and compatible with the two approved assets, rather than like a commercial UI mockup or realistic furniture render.

COLOR DIRECTION

Frame:
Muted oak brown, warm taupe and deeper brown shadows.

Inner surface:
Light cream, slightly warm beige.

Outlines:
Dark warm brown, consistent with the approved masters.

Keep the interior surface brighter and quieter than the outer frame.

COMPOSITION AND OUTPUT

- Exactly one complete board.
- Entire board fully contained within the image canvas.
- Maintain at least 5% transparent safe margin on every side.
- Center the board visually.
- Request genuine transparent background.
- No floor, wall, room or environmental scenery.
- No environmental shadow outside the object.
- Clean transparent edges.
- High enough image quality for homepage use.
- The final file resolution and alpha quality must be independently verified after generation.

CRITICAL UI CONSTRAINTS

The board is ARTWORK ONLY.

All future UI must be created separately as editable Figma layers or real frontend components.

Therefore, DO NOT render:
- Any letters, words, numbers or labels.
- Create or Import text.
- Buttons or button shapes.
- Icons or icon placeholders.
- Project thumbnails or project cards.
- Lists, dividers, input fields or checkboxes.
- Decorative UI panels or reserved interface boxes.
- Textured papers, pinned notes or notices.
- Fake interface layouts.

The inner board must remain one uninterrupted neutral surface.

STRICT EXCLUSIONS

No raccoon.
No washing machine.
No plants.
No Minecraft blocks.
No attached decorations.
No hanging ropes or chains.
No supporting legs or easel.
No paper sheets, stickers, pins or tape.
No additional boards.
No room, floor, wall or scenery.
No text or watermark.
No background color.
No cropped parts.
No collage, patchwork or reconstruction of missing fragments.
No photorealism.
No glossy 3D rendering.
No exaggerated perspective.
No heavy grain or complex wood patterns.

ANIMATION AND LAYER READINESS

This is one complete visual master, not a request for separate editable layers.

Keep the frame and inner panel clearly distinguishable so they can be recreated as separate Figma layers later if needed.

Do not claim that a flat PNG contains editable internal components.

ACCEPTANCE TARGET

Create a complete, charming and structurally coherent wooden board that fits naturally beside the approved Raccoon and Washer masters.

It must provide a large, clean, uninterrupted front-facing surface suitable for overlaying editable homepage UI later.

No visual reconstruction, fragment assembly or repainting of missing areas should be required.

Generate the illustration only.
